Linguistic Feature Engineering

Seven handcrafted features capture stylistic signatures of fake news

FeatureFake News PatternReal News Pattern
CAPS RatioHigh — ALL CAPS for emphasisLow — sentence case
Exclamation RatioHigh — frequent !!Low — rare punctuation
Sensational ScoreHigh — "shocking", "exposed"Low — neutral language
Credibility ScoreLow — no attributionHigh — "according to", "study"
Avg Word LengthShorter — punchy wordsLonger — technical terms
Text LengthVariable — often shortModerate — structured
Question RatioHigher — rhetorical questionsLower — declarative statements
